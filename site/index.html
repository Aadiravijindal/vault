    The write path

    A lie only has to get written once.

    An agent reads an email, a ticket, a web page — then writes what it learned somewhere it will read again tomorrow. No identity check. No judgment about the source. No record it happened.

    Then a guess an AI made on Tuesday gets read back on Friday as company policy.

    Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 showed hidden web-page text writing itself into an agent's long-term memory, where it survived into every later session. OWASP lists it as ASI06.

    The gate

    Ten checks. Every write. No off switch.

    Pass, hold and block are all logged the same way — the blocked write is the one the auditor asks about.

    01 / 10 — Identity

    Who is writing?

    Registered agent, valid credential, expected origin, allowed to write here. An unknown agent gets blocked and raises an alert.

    02 / 10 — Channel trust

    Where did it come from?

    Fourteen tiers, decided by architecture and not by a filter's opinion. A logged-in employee is trusted. Email isn't — including internal email.

    03 / 10 — Source

    Is the sender real?

    SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Webhook signatures. mTLS. Lookalike and homoglyph domains caught before anything downstream reads them.

    04 / 10 — Private information

    45 detectors.

    Cards, IBAN, SSN, Aadhaar, medical records, cloud keys, private keys. Credentials are blocked outright — never masked and stored.

    05 / 10 — Sensitivity

    When unsure, label higher.

    Public, internal, confidential, secret, or your own taxonomy. Uncertainty goes up and out to a person. It never guesses downward.

    06 / 10 — Walls

    Sales can't leak into legal.

    Departments and projects stay isolated on read and write. No admin bypass. Break-glass takes two named humans and a time box.

    07 / 10 — Instructions

    Facts in. Commands out.

    A fact describes the world; an instruction tries to change your agent. Seven layers run together, and any one of them firing holds the write.

    08 / 10 — Rules

    Backtest before you enable.

    Write the rule in plain English. See exactly what it would have blocked over the last 90 days, and what it costs your reviewers, before it's on.

    09 / 10 — Reconciliation

    Authority beats recency.

    Contradictions resolve by who has standing, not by what arrived last. The losing fact moves to history. It is never deleted.

    10 / 10 — Consent

    Are you allowed to keep it?

    Identifiable person, recorded lawful basis, still valid, not under erasure. No basis means hold and route to Privacy. It doesn't guess.

    Proof

    The record isn't ours to be trusted with.

    Every write, block, read and admin action lands in an append-only hash chain — and you hold the signing keys. A standalone verifier, importing nothing from our code, lets your auditor check it while trusting neither of us.

    • Trace

      Click any fact.

      Who said it, which agent captured it, every check and its verdict, who reviewed it, and everything built on top of it since.

    • Undo

      Roll it back.

      One fact, one agent, one source, one folder, or the whole memory — to any point in time. Nothing is destroyed.

    • Contagion

      Who believed it?

      Every agent that read it, every fact derived from it, the blast radius, and one click to reverse all of it.

    • Bundle

      One click, one file.

      The fact, its trace, the raw source, the chain proof and a plain-language timeline — for a regulator, insurer or opposing counsel.
